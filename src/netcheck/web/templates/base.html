Privacy

We only scan the internet address you're connecting from, and only when you click the button. We don't keep your IP address: for rate limiting we store a scrambled version that we can't reverse, and delete it within a day. Results are kept for 24 hours at a private link, then deleted. Results can include names your devices broadcast (like a router model), so share the link with care. To check your address's reputation and history we look it up with {{ lookup_services }}. We keep only daily totals of results (how many were green or red), never who got them. There are no ads, analytics or trackers.

This site is open source: read the code on GitHub.
