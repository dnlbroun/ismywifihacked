How the check works

We look at your connection from the outside, the way a stranger on the internet would. Here's exactly what that means, and what it can't tell you.

What we check

Open doors
We try to connect to about 30 common entry points (router admin pages, remote desktop, file sharing, cameras, printers, Android TV boxes and more), and ask whether your router answers UPnP, NAT-PMP or DNS requests from the internet. We only check whether something answers. We never try passwords or break in. If your connection has both an IPv4 and an IPv6 address, we check both.
DNS hijacking
Your browser looks up a random one-time name on our own DNS server, so we can see which DNS service answered for you, and whether it's your provider, a well-known public service, or something unexpected.
Botnet and abuse reports
We look up your address with GreyNoise (has it been seen scanning the internet?) and AbuseIPDB (has anyone reported it for attacks?).
History
We ask Shodan, a search engine for internet-connected devices, what it has seen open on your address in the past week, including things our quick check doesn't cover.

What the colours mean

  • Looks good We checked, and found nothing wrong.
  • Nothing visible Your network kept us out. That's usually a good sign, but we can't call it a pass.
  • Worth a look Something is reachable or reported that may be fine if you set it up on purpose.
  • Fix this Something risky is exposed, with steps to fix it.
  • Couldn't check A check failed or a service didn't answer. We never turn that into a pass.

Privacy

  • We only scan the address you're connecting from, and only after you click "Scan me". There's no way to point the scan at anyone else.
  • We don't store your IP address. For rate limiting we keep a scrambled version we can't reverse, and drop it within a day.
  • Results live at a private link for 24 hours, then they're deleted. We keep only daily totals (how many results were green or red), never who got them.
  • To check reputation and history, we send your address to {{ lookup_services }}.
  • No ads, analytics, trackers or accounts.
