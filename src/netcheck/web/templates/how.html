How it works

What we check, where the information comes from, and what we can't see.

The checks

Exposed devices
We try about 30 entry points (router admin pages, remote desktop, file sharing, cameras, printers, Android TV boxes) and ask whether your router answers UPnP, NAT-PMP or DNS requests from outside. We only note what answers and never log in. If you have both an IPv4 and an IPv6 address, we check both.
DNS hijacking
Your browser looks up a one-time name on our DNS server, which shows us which DNS service answered for you.
Botnet activity
We ask GreyNoise whether your address has been seen scanning the internet, and AbuseIPDB whether it's been reported for attacks.
History
We ask Shodan what it has seen open on your address in the past week.

The colours

  • Looks good Checked, nothing wrong.
  • Nothing visible Your network kept us out. Usually good, but not a pass.
  • Worth a look Fine if you set it up on purpose.
  • Fix this Something risky is exposed or reported.
  • Couldn't check A check failed. We never count that as a pass.

Privacy

  • We only scan the address you connect from, and only after you click "Scan me".
  • We don't store your IP address. Rate limiting uses a scrambled form that's dropped within a day.
  • Results are deleted after 24 hours. We keep daily totals of results, never who got them.
  • We send your address to {{ lookup_services }} to check its reputation.
  • No ads, analytics, trackers or accounts. The code is on GitHub.
