Only scan a connection that's yours or that you're allowed to test. On shared Wi-Fi (office, café, apartment building), the scan covers everyone on it.

What we check

  • Exposed devices. Whether your router, cameras, file shares or other devices can be reached from the internet. We try about 30 common ports.
  • DNS hijacking. Whether your web lookups go through a server you didn't choose.
  • Botnet activity. Whether your address has been seen or reported attacking other computers.

We never try passwords or log in to anything. To check things a website can't see, like who's on your Wi-Fi, use the Wi-Fi guide.
