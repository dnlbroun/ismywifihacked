Is my Wi-Fi hacked?

We check your connection from the outside, the way an attacker would: for open doors into your network, hijacked DNS, and signs of an infected device. It takes about 15 seconds.

Only scan a connection that's yours or that you're allowed to test. On shared Wi-Fi (office, café, apartment building), the scan covers everyone on it.

What we check

  • Open doors. About 30 entry points attackers try: router admin pages, cameras, file sharing, remote desktop, UPnP.
  • DNS hijacking. Whether your lookups go somewhere unexpected.
  • Botnet activity. Whether your address has been seen or reported attacking other computers.

We never try passwords. We only see what answers. For what a website can't see, like who's on your Wi-Fi, use the Wi-Fi guide.
